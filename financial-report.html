I. Total Collection & Allocation Overview

During the reporting period of July 1 to July 31, 2026, the Spread Love Foundation received a total of $487,350.00 in unrestricted and designated donations from 3,842 individual donors, 14 corporate partners, and 3 institutional grants. These funds were collected through our secure online portal, direct bank transfers, cryptocurrency channels, and on-the-ground cash collections coordinated by our regional partners in Amman and Cairo. Every contribution, regardless of size, has been logged, receipted, and allocated in accordance with our mandate of maximum field impact with zero administrative waste.

The collected sum was deployed across four primary intervention pillars. Food Security & Nutrition received the largest allocation at $182,250 (37.4%), funding the procurement, packaging, and last-mile distribution of emergency food parcels, infant formula, and therapeutic nutrition supplements for malnourished children under five. Emergency Medical Aid was allocated $121,840 (25.0%), covering surgical instrument kits, trauma dressings, antibiotics, anesthetic supplies, and the operational costs of two mobile clinics serving displaced communities in southern Gaza. Shelter & Non-Food Items received $97,470 (20.0%), enabling the construction of weather-resistant temporary shelters and the distribution of thermal blankets, hygiene kits, and solar-powered lighting units. The remaining $85,790 (17.6%) was directed to Logistics & Operational Support, including fuel for our fleet of twelve aid trucks, driver per-diems, warehouse rentals in Rafah and Deir al-Balah, communications equipment, and security coordination fees necessary to navigate access corridors safely.

Of the total funds raised, $487,350 has been fully disbursed. There was no remaining unallocated balance at the close of the reporting period. However, it is worth noting that an additional $12,400 carried over from June 2026 was utilized during the first week of July to pre-position fuel reserves and secure warehouse space ahead of the Ramadan distribution surge. This rollover was approved by the Board Finance Committee on June 28, 2026, and is documented in Addendum A of this report. All procurement was conducted through competitive bidding with pre-qualified vendors, and every delivery was photographed, geo-tagged, and cross-checked against beneficiary registration rolls by our independent monitoring partner, Veritas Field Analytics.

III. Forward Outlook & Call to Action

In the preceding month of June 2026, the Foundation raised $412,180 and delivered 11,800 food parcels, 520 medical kits, and shelter for 290 families. The month-on-month growth of 18.2% in total donations reflects the growing trust of our donor community and the expanding visibility of our field operations. We are deeply grateful to every individual who has chosen to stand with us.

Looking ahead to August and September 2026, we have four critical initiatives on the horizon that require sustained funding. First, the Winter Preparedness Program will pre-position 800 additional thermal shelter kits and 5,000 winter clothing bundles before temperatures drop. Second, our Mobile Medical Expansion aims to deploy a third mobile clinic to northern Gaza, where access to healthcare remains severely constrained. Third, the School Reopening Support initiative will provide educational materials, temporary classroom tents, and psychosocial support to 1,200 children in time for the new academic year. Finally, our Water & Sanitation project will install 24 portable water purification units and 180 family-level filtration systems in camps currently relying on untreated sources. The total projected budget for these programs is $640,000, of which $198,000 has already been pledged by returning donors.

The need on the ground has never been more urgent, but neither has the opportunity to make a tangible difference. Every dollar you contribute goes directly into the hands of families who have lost everything except their dignity and their hope. We invite you to renew your support, increase your monthly pledge, or share our mission with someone who believes, as you do, that compassion is not a feeling but an action. Together, we are not merely responding to a crisis; we are rebuilding lives, one meal, one shelter, one child at a time.
