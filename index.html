WHO WE ARE

About Spread Love Foundation

Spread Love Foundation was founded by a small group of aid workers who refused to look away. What began as a single truck of blankets and canned food has grown into a full field operation delivering meals, medical care, and shelter to families displaced by conflict.

We work directly with local volunteers and partner organizations on the ground, so your donation moves fast and reaches the people who need it most — no bureaucracy, no delays.

OUR MISSION IN ACTION

What We Do

Urgent Medical Infrastructure

Hospitals are at breaking point. We provide life-saving surgical kits, mobile clinics, and critical medicine. Without these supplies, simple injuries become fatal.

They cannot wait another day.

LEARN ABOUT MEDICAL RELIEF

Critical Food Security

Starvation is a weapon of war. We are on the ground delivering calorie-dense food baskets to thousands of families who haven't had a warm meal in weeks.

Every $25 provides a family meal kit for 2 weeks.

Dignified Shelter

Winter is coming. For those who lost their homes, a waterproof tent and thermal blanket aren't just comforts—they are survival.

Healing the Next Generation

Over 400 orphans are under our direct care. We provide psychological support, safe housing, and education. We don't just help them survive; we help them heal.

If we don't protect them, who will?
